Project overview

What we're building

A structured pruning + activation healing pipeline (TIM-MGDT) that identifies which transformer layers are elastic vs critical, prunes the elastic ones, and heals the activation drift — recovering perplexity while cutting parameters, latency, and memory by ~50%.

Why this matters

Existing pruning methods either destroy quality or require expensive retraining. We discovered that Layer 10 in GPT-2 is elastic, not broken — the +108% PPL explosion on hard removal shows over-weighting, not architectural damage. A 2% scaling fix restores balance. TIM-MGDT exploits this insight systematically.

Where we are today

Phase 11 complete — real GPU validation on GPT-2 (124M). Found a 30% sparsity Winning Ticket (+4.9% PPL only). Discovered that activation healing hurts GPT-2 (Conv1D architecture) but will massively help Llama/Mistral (nn.Linear). Next: 7B model runs on dual 3090s.

The research question

Can temporal importance metrics — cheap proxy signals computed from gradient dynamics — reliably identify and exploit layer-level elasticity to achieve lossless (< 5% PPL) structured compression at 50%+ parameter reduction without retraining?

Key decisions
TopicDecisionRationale
Architecture insightLayer 10 is elastic, not broken +108% PPL on hard removal = over-weighting. 2% scaling restores it. This insight drives the entire compression strategy.
Winning Ticket30% unstructured sparsity (GPT-2) +4.9% PPL only. Above 30%, PPL degrades rapidly (+21.3% at 40%) — classic phase transition boundary verified.
Activation HealingConv1D blocks immune → nn.Linear blocks will benefit massively GPT-2 intermediate layers use Conv1D (unpruned by global linear pruning). Healing added noise. Llama/Mistral use nn.Linear everywhere — healing will recover massive quality loss.
Head PruningMHA is destructive; GQA will be far more tolerant GPT-2 MHA: +59.3% PPL at 20% head pruning. Llama/Mistral GQA (8 KV groups for 32 Q heads) has built-in query redundancy → much safer.
Eval metricPerplexity on WikiText-2 (PPL) Standard LM evaluation. Spearman ρ > 0.7 for proxy-to-ground-truth correlation. P-value < 0.05 for statistical significance.
ComputeApple Silicon (quick runs) → 2×3090 (48 GB, full runs) Two-phase compute strategy. Remote SSH to dual 3090 for 7B models. See REMOTE_SETUP_GUIDE.md.

Literature & Related Work
Paper / AuthorsContext & Our Differentiation
SparseGPT
Frantar et al., 2023		One-shot pruning using Hessian inverse. High VRAM requirement. Our diff: We use cheap 1st-order gradient dynamics instead of expensive 2nd-order Hessians.
Wanda
Sun et al., 2023		Prunes based on weight magnitude × input activation norm. Our diff: We use temporal gradient variance to find elasticity, avoiding need for large calibration sets.
SliceGPT
Ashkboos et al., 2024		Deletes entire rows/cols and uses PCA rotation. Our diff: We prune without destroying the original parameter space mapping, enabling forward-hook healing.
ShortGPT
Men et al., 2024		Removes entire blocks based on block-level redundancy. Our diff: We do fine-grained structured/unstructured pruning, rather than taking out whole transformer blocks.
