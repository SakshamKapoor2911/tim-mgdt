Core Claim
----------

Reasoning collapse in compressed Mistral-7B is not explained by parameter sparsity or modest C4 perplexity degradation alone. Dense-shaped unstructured magnitude masks preserve GSM8K reasoning at high sparsity, while hardware-aligned structural MLP-width pruning destroys GSM8K at much lower sparsity.
