Core Claim
----------

Reasoning collapse in compressed Mistral-7B is not explained by parameter sparsity or modest C4 perplexity degradation alone. Dense-shaped unstructured magnitude masks preserve GSM8K reasoning at high sparsity, while hardware-aligned structural MLP-width pruning destroys GSM8K at much lower sparsity.

Key contrast to preserve:

- Phase 16 structural 20% width pruning: full GSM8K collapses to roughly 5%.
- Phase 18B structural 1-5% width pruning: smoke GSM8K drops sharply even at very low structural sparsity.
- Phase 19 dense-shaped 20% mask: full GSM8K remains within 1.44 points of dense.
- Phase 19 dense-shaped 25%, 30%, and 40% masks: full GSM8K degrades to 34.04%, 31.77%, and 23.88%, locating the mask-retention threshold just above 20%.
- Phase 21 auxiliary reasoning smoke: the 20% mask is close to dense on GSM8K Platinum strict EM (34% versus 36%, n=100) and above dense on BBH multistep arithmetic exact match (39% versus 33%, n=100). Treat this as support, not the primary claim.
- Phase 22 cross-model smoke: on Llama-3-8B GSM8K smoke (n=100), a 10% dense-shaped mask reaches 49% strict EM versus 53% dense, while a 20% mask drops to 29%. Treat this as threshold heterogeneity, not a full cross-model benchmark.

Running Evidence
----------------

Adaptive boundary and structural follow-up runs:

- `phase19_adaptive_boundary`: completed the 25% branch; 25% is already materially degraded.
- `phase20_mask_guided_queue`: will now skip larger points because initial mask-guided structured pruning failed its smoke continuation gate.

Current interpretation:

- The result is thresholded rather than broad: 20% is near dense, 25% is already degraded, and 30%/40% collapse further.
- This is still publishable as a mask-vs-structure dissociation because structural width pruning fails at much lower sparsity.
- Phase 20 is currently negative evidence: naive mask-guided structural transfer fails even below 1% MLP sparsity.
- Phase 22 prevents an overclaim: the Mistral 20% threshold should be reported as model-specific until a broader sweep is run.
